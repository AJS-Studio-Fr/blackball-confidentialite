Local play and online features

Billard Français By AJS Studio (formerly Carom By AJS Studio) · Android · app.ajsstudio.carambole

On the phone

Profiles, results, history, settings, practice progress and resumed matches are stored locally. Import and export transfer your own backups. Exported files are not erased when the app is removed.

Google sign-in, friendly matches and clubs

If you use online features, Google and Firebase Authentication process your name, email address and account ID. Firebase Realtime Database uses your nickname, club, availability, invitations, shots and match state to connect players and synchronize matches. The players involved can see your nickname and the information needed for the match.

Cloud backup

When you start a backup, Firestore stores a private copy linked to your account. It may contain profiles, results, progress, saved positions, settings and resumed matches. Local modes are available without an account.

Advertising and consent

Version 0.1.29 and later may show Google AdMob ads in the main menu and when returning from a completed offline match. Ads are never shown during play.

To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available. Google may infer an approximate location from the IP address.

Where advertising consent is required, the message lets you accept, decline or manage your choices. To change them, open the main menu and select “Ad privacy options” when the button is available. Learn more about how Google uses data on partner sites and apps.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your phone until erased. Exported files remain under your control. Cloud backups stay available until deleted or the account is closed. To delete your game account, open Account and backups in Settings. Google may ask you to sign in again. This removes the backup and the private data covered by the deletion process. Account closure markers and references in shared matches may remain, including an ID or nickname. Use the link below or email contact@ajsstudio.fr to request deletion of those references or other associated data. Specify Billard Français By AJS Studio (formerly Carom By AJS Studio) and your sign-in email.

Security and hosting

Traffic to Firebase is encrypted in transit. Realtime Database and Firestore are configured in Europe. Google may process IP addresses and technical logs needed to provide the service. Firebase Authentication keeps logged IP addresses for a few weeks and may take up to 180 days to remove other authentication information from its systems after deletion is initiated.

Your rights and requests

You may request access, correction, erasure or restriction of processing and, where applicable, exercise rights to object and data portability. Consent may be withdrawn for the future without affecting earlier lawful processing. An Android permission does not necessarily constitute consent to advertising use.

Specify the application and data concerned. Where necessary, we will request only the information needed to verify account ownership. Never send a password or sign-in code.

Requests must receive a response without undue delay, normally within one month. An extension of two months is possible for complex or numerous requests, with notice within the first month. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

Providers and transfers

When using the Google/Firebase features described on this page, these providers process information needed for the service, including IP addresses and technical connection data. A European database location does not guarantee all processing stays in Europe. See Firebase privacy and transfer information and Google’s privacy policy.

The website is hosted by OVHcloud. Email requests are handled by AJS Studio and the mail services needed to deliver them. Website information is described separately in the website privacy notice.
